# ACME Global Employee Handbook & Workplace Guidelines

## 1. Remote and Hybrid Work Framework
ACME operates on a modern hybrid work framework designed to balance flexible working styles with team collaboration:
- **Eligible Roles**: Most engineering, product, and design roles are eligible for hybrid work.
- **Office vs Remote Days**: Team members may work remotely up to two (2) days per week.
- **Core Collaboration Hours**: All employees are requested to remain available and reachable on Slack/Teams between **10:00 AM and 4:00 PM** local time.
- **Monthly Home Office Allowance**: Employees working remotely receive a monthly high-speed internet stipend of **$50**, automatically credited on the monthly payroll.

## 2. Hardware and Equipment Standard
Upon joining, each employee is provided with:
1. One high-performance workstation: Choice between an Apple MacBook Pro 16" (M-series) or a Dell XPS 15 (Windows/Linux).
2. A dual 27-inch 4K monitor setup for home or office.
3. An ergonomic chair subsidy up to $300 (one-time upon onboarding).

All equipment remains company property and must be returned upon cessation of employment.

## 3. Professional Conduct and Workplace Culture
We cultivate an inclusive, psychologically safe, and harassment-free environment:
- **Zero Tolerance Policy**: Any form of discrimination, sexual harassment, or bullying results in immediate disciplinary review up to termination.
- **Intellectual Property**: All software, documentation, and inventions authored during working hours are the exclusive intellectual property of ACME Global Corp.
