ACME GLOBAL CORP - COMPREHENSIVE LEAVE & TIME OFF POLICY
Document Version: 2026.2
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
All regular full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues on a pro-rata basis at a rate of 1.67 days per full calendar month of service. Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis calculated according to their contractual hours.

2. CARRY-OVER RULES
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be utilized within the first quarter (by March 31) of the subsequent year, after which they will lapse without cash compensation.

3. SICK AND MEDICAL LEAVE
Employees are granted up to 10 days of fully paid sick leave each calendar year. For any sickness absence exceeding three consecutive working days, an official medical certificate signed by a licensed healthcare professional must be submitted to the HR portal within 48 hours of return.

4. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth or legal adoption of a child. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave may be taken continuously or in two separate blocks within the first 12 months.

5. BEREAVEMENT AND EMERGENCY LEAVE
Employees may take up to 5 consecutive paid days of compassionate leave in the event of the loss of an immediate family member. Up to 2 paid personal emergency days are also provided annually for unforeseen domestic emergencies.
